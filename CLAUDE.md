# Koda — map for Claude

A YouTube Music client in SwiftUI: an iPhone app plus a native Mac app (SwiftUI on AppKit, macOS 26, universal; Catalyst was dropped in 0.4). iOS 26 deployment target, Liquid Glass. Swift 6, `@Observable`, no third-party packages. The Apple sibling of Koda for Android (`E:\Ivors\Developement\open-source\Koda`), built for Apple's platforms rather than ported: copy Android's ideas where they serve users here, never its layouts or habits.

## Rules

- **Keep this file current.** Every change that adds a feature, file, setting, persisted key or non-obvious decision adds or updates a short line here: where it lives and how it works. Short entries, no history; history goes in commits, Mac design notes in `docs/mac.md`.
- **Build it the way Apple would.** Before designing any screen or interaction, ask how Apple would have made it in its own apps on that platform (Music, TV, Podcasts, Mail, Finder, System Settings) and do that. A Mac app is not an iPhone app in a window: Mac apps put everything in the sidebar instead of switching modes, open side panels with `.inspector`, open new windows, and give every place a menu-bar command and a keyboard shortcut.
- **The most default SwiftUI that does the job.** System controls, materials, transitions and APIs (`safeAreaBar`, `scrollEdgeEffectStyle`, `navigationTransition(.zoom)`, `ContentUnavailableView`, `.glassEffect`) over hand-rolled ones. No hacks to fight the framework. Reach for the system's own component first (`List(.sidebar)` with `Section(isExpanded:)`, `.inspector`, `NavigationSplitView`, `Picker(.segmented)`, `.searchable`, `WindowGroup`, `ContentUnavailableView`) and draw something custom only when no system component can do it. Native SwiftUI is the bar Koda is held to.
- **iPhone UI stays as it is** unless the task is about it. The Mac has its own views; shared screens branch with `if Platform.isMac { macBody } else { phoneBody }`.
- New `.swift` files are picked up automatically (synchronized folders); no pbxproj edits needed.
- The app target defaults to **MainActor isolation**: value types used off the main actor (Codable snapshots, anything sent to `Task.detached`) need `nonisolated struct/enum`.
- Comments explain *why*, in plain sentences, matching the existing style.

## How things work

- **Environment:** `AppModel` owns `Player`, `Library`, `Downloads`, `LastFM`, `settings`. Views read them with `@Environment(X.self)`. Anything *presented* (sheet, cover, popover) must add `.appEnvironment(model)` (it crashed on Catalyst; kept so every presentation has the full environment).
- **Settings:** add a property to `AppSettings` with a `didSet` that writes UserDefaults (`koda.*` keys), read it in `init`, then show it in both `Screens/SettingsView.swift` (iPhone Form) and `Mac/MacSettingsView.swift` (pane, `MacSettingsGroup`/`MacSettingsToggle`/`MacSettingsRow`). Player-owned prefs (autoplay, normalize, cache while streaming, volume) live on `Player`.
- **Navigation:** push with `NavigationLink(value: Route.…)`; from outside a stack use `model.open(route)`. The Mac keeps one path per sidebar section (`paths[MacSection]`); Now Playing on the Mac is `Route.nowPlaying` pushed with the zoom transition.
- **Mac toolbar:** never hide/remove the toolbar or sidebar toggle at runtime; change what the detail column shows instead (see `docs/mac.md`).
- **Loading:** pages use `LoadState` and draw their own layout redacted from a placeholder while loading (`.loadingPlaceholder()`).
- **Cold launch:** `HomeModel` restores its last `Snapshot` ("home") in `init` and refreshes in place when `needsRefresh`; Search's mood tiles restore from "moods". To make another page open from cache, give it a Codable state and use `Snapshot.load/save` the same way.
- **Artwork:** always `ArtworkView` (square crop; YouTube video thumbnails are letterboxed 4:3 and are scaled to hide the bars). Images that fill a frame go in an `.overlay` of a fixed shape so their own size never drives layout.
- **Skip buttons:** `AppSettings.showsSkipButtons` / `skipInterval` (5/10/15/30 s, `gobackward.N`/`goforward.N` symbols) → `Player.skip(by:)`. Shown in `TransportControls` (Now Playing) and `MacPlayerBar`; ⌥⌘←/→ in the Controls menu. Lock-screen skip commands stay off so next/previous remain there.
- **Volume bar (iPhone):** `VolumeSlider` (Player/Scrubber.swift) sets the system volume through the slider of a near-invisible `MPVolumeView`, looked up on every change (a slider found once went stale with Bluetooth headphones, most likely rebuilt on a route change), and settles to `outputVolume` 0.6 s after the last change. The Mac uses `Player.volume`.
- **Mac sidebar:** one sidebar for everything, no mode switch on the Mac: collapsible `Section(isExpanded:)` groups Music (Search first, as Music's: `MacSection.search` shows moods/recent searches until something is searched, then results, and focuses the toolbar field; `.browse` is the same page), Library, Your Playlists, then with Video mode on Videos (Home, Subscriptions, Search Results, Watch History, Watch Later, Liked Videos), Channels and Explore (folded by default). Open/closed is kept in `@AppStorage("koda.mac.sidebar.<group>")`. The selected place sets `AppSettings.mode` (so a launch reopens that side; `section.isVideo` picks the toolbar search's target); Go menu: Home ⌘1, Library ⌘2, Videos ⌘3, Watch History ⌘4, opening a folded group. The player bar is whatever is playing (`showsVideoBar`: a video away from its page that plays, or no song, or both paused and you're in a video place), never hidden by where you are.
- **Lyrics and Up Next beside the page (Mac):** the player bar's Lyrics and Up Next buttons (and Controls → Lyrics/Queue, the glance's Show All) set `AppModel.sidePanel` (`MacSidePanel`), shown with SwiftUI's `.inspector` on the detail column: one at a time, a segmented Lyrics | Up Next switch at its top, under the window's toolbar (it once sat up in the toolbar band, ignoring the top safe area: there it couldn't be clicked, since Catalyst gives the band to the window for dragging, and the sidebar button vanished after hiding the sidebar with the panel open; not a toolbar item either, the toolbar never changes at runtime), the same button again closes it, in the window's own appearance (the panels are Now Playing's `LyricsPanel`/`QueuePanel`, drawn with semantic colours so they read light here and on Now Playing's forced-dark colours; `PlayerToggleStyle` draws in plain black/white picked from `colorScheme`: the `.background` and hierarchical `.primary` styles resolved to the inspector's translucent material, and the toggles faded into it). The panel's translucent background blends its text, which ignored each lyric line's own fade (only blurred lines, flattened by the blur, faded), so `LyricRow` is a `.compositingGroup()` before its opacity. A word-timed line swaps its resting copy for `KaraokeLine` with `.transition(.identity)`: crossfading under the row's spring lit the whole line before it filled. It steps aside while Now Playing is open (which has its own) and closes when nothing is playing. The bar's cover opens Now Playing. `toggleLyricsOrQueue` opens Now Playing's panel on the iPhone. Covers in the mini players are concentric with their bar (Mac 46 pt / 12, iPhone 36 / 10).
- **Sidebar foot (Mac):** `MacSidebarGlance` shows the next song and today's listening, attached with `safeAreaBar` so the list gets the system scroll-edge effect under it.
- **Crossfade:** `Player.crossfades` / `crossfadeDuration` (default on, 5 s, 1–12). With it on, the next song waits on a separate paused `AVQueuePlayer` (`standby`) instead of behind the current item; a boundary observer at end − duration calls `switchToStandby`, which retires the old player to `tail` (fading out, equal-power) and adopts the standby as `avPlayer` (`attach`/`detach` move the KVO + time observers; callbacks from a non-main player are dropped). Skips fade by `Player.skipFade` (0.6 s). AVFoundation can't move an item between players (`_attachToPlayer` throws), hence two players. Off, the old gapless queue path is unchanged. Repeat one crossfades into the song's own start (the standby holds the same occurrence); a preloaded copy of the current occurrence becoming current is a loop, which resets `startedReported` so history, stats and Last.fm count a new play. `sampleTime` republishes Now Playing whenever a sample changes the rate or drifts > 1 s, or the Lock Screen keeps a stale (frozen) clock. Live tests: `KODA_LIVE=1 swift test --filter LivePlayerTests`.
- **AutoMix:** `Player.autoMix` (`koda.autoMix`, off by default) / `Player.transitions` (AutoMix, Crossfade, Off: Settings → Song Transitions on both platforms; AutoMix toggle in the queue panel). Uses the crossfade standby path (`usesStandby`). `Playback/AutoMix/`: `AutoMixAnalyzer` (actor) decodes each song (AudioStore file once `CachingLoader` has it whole, else a one-off download) to mono 22.05 kHz with AVAssetReader and runs `TrackAnalyzer` (music start/end, body level, ending fade/hard/decay, fade −8 dB cue, intro length, tempo by onset autocorrelation, local intro/outro `BeatGrid`s), cached as JSON in `<storage>/AutoMix/<videoId>.json` (`TrackAnalysis.currentVersion` invalidates). `TransitionPlanner` picks gapless (album neighbours, unshuffled), beatMatch (tempos within 6 %, strong grids: 8–32 beats ending on the last beat, bass swap mid-way), fadeRide, filterBlend or quickBlend, and snaps any plan to beats when it can. An early beat-matched mix at an outro section may leave out at most `maxLeftOut` (8 s) of audible music after it, else the song plays to its ending (soft final sections like WILDFLOWER's were being cut; beat strength can't tell them apart). quickBlend (hard stop, tempos too far apart) brings the next in on a beat ~3 s before the stop, old song's bass out, new one's in. `AutoMixStudy` (`KODA_STUDY=<folder>`, folder holds manifest.json) measures a set of real songs and plans every pair to JSON; fixture `automix-songs.json` holds real measurements for tests. `TransitionDSP` is an `MTAudioProcessingTap` per AVPlayerItem (attached before the item plays) applying the plan's `Automation` (gain, high-pass, low-pass) by item time. `startAutoMix` fires `autoMixLead` before the cue and starts the standby with `setRate(_:time:atHostTime:)` at the cue's host time (standby has `automaticallyWaitsToMinimizeStalling = false`), at the plan's tempo-matching rate, which it keeps until it next starts from a standstill (pause/play, a load): **never change a playing AVPlayer's rate or `automaticallyWaitsToMinimizeStalling`**. On iOS 27 each such change makes `mediaplaybackd` begin an interruption and rebuild the route with no end, and the player goes on "playing" in silence (seen live with `idevicesyslog`: easing Espresso from 0.9995 to 1 after the blend). So the planner leaves tempos alone when the beats drift < `inaudibleDrift` (10 ms) over the blend, plans from the outgoing song's real rate (`Context.outgoingRate` ← `Player.heardRate`; plan `rate` is incoming seconds per outgoing second, the player plays `rate × outgoingRate`, back to its own tempo whenever the beats allow, never past `maxStretch` from it), and `PlanKey` carries that rate so a song resumed at 1 is replanned. `Player.mixing` drives `MixingBadge` (under the Now Playing scrubber, which also fades the old song's bar; Mac player bar) and `MixingCovers` (old cover dissolves into the new over the overlap, faint pulse on locked beats). Everything is logged by `AutoMixLog`, along with what stops the sound (pause/play, audio interruptions with reason, route changes with the outputs, the main player stopping or waiting while `wantsToPlay`, then how long until it played again, stalls) (Console: subsystem Koda, category AutoMix; stdout; `~/Library/Application Support/Koda/automix.log` on the Mac). On the iPhone read it live with `idevicesyslog` (see the Windows PC notes). Debug builds: Controls → Jump to Next Transition (⌥⌘J). Tests: `AutoMixTests`; `KODA_LIVE=1 swift test --filter LiveAutoMixTests`.
- **Animated Art:** `AppSettings.animatedArt` (`koda.player.animatedArt`: On / Wi‑Fi Only (default) / Off; Settings → Look and Feel, Mac Appearance pane), as Music's. `AppModel.motionArtwork` (`MotionArtwork`, Caches/MotionArtwork) searches Apple Music's catalogue (`amp-api-edge`, storefront us) with the anonymous token scraped from Music on the web's script bundle (issuer AMPWebPlay, kept in a file until an hour before it expires; redirects only within music.apple.com), as Koda for Android does. A result counts only if title (feat. stripped), artist, album (" - Single"/" - EP" stripped) and length (±8 s) all agree, and only songs with an album and not videos are looked up: a wrong moving cover is worse than the still one. Each loop is one fragmented MP4 cut into byte ranges by its HLS media playlist, so the chosen rung (smallest width ≥ 85 % of the ask, ≤ 1080, lowest AVERAGE-BANDWIDTH; ~6.5 MB for 960 px) is downloaded whole as `<appleAlbumID>-<square|tall>-<width>.mp4` (250 MB, least recently shown dropped) and plays locally. Cached hard so a song is asked about rarely: every lookup is kept in lookups.json (found 180 days, none 14 days), a found cover is also kept under its YouTube album (`album:<MPRE id>`) so every song on the album gets it without a search, answers are read by song id before anything else (a song can reach the player without its album), and a song with no album is asked about once through `YouTubeMusic.songDetails` (a plain `next` for the video; `MotionArtwork(findAlbum:)`, a throw is not remembered, "on no album" is), loops and the Lock Screen's still frames (`previewImage`, `<appleAlbumID>-<shape>-preview.jpg`) share the 500 MB budget, least recently shown dropped. Kept loops play offline; Wi‑Fi Only = `allowsExpensiveNetworkAccess = false`, never in Low Data Mode. `AnimatedCover` (Player/AnimatedCover.swift, `shape` square or tall) lays a muted `AVPlayerLooper` over the big cover in `MixingCovers` (Now Playing, both platforms; not the compact header), fading in on `isReadyForDisplay`, frozen while paused, off with Accessibility's animated images off; the loop player never prevents display sleep, AirPlays or touches the audio session. **Full-screen art (iPhone):** with the cover page open and the album having a tall loop, `FullScreenMotionArt` plays it across the top of Now Playing (screen width × 4/3, under the status bar), its lower part under `.ultraThinMaterial` faded in by a gradient and the whole melting into the mesh background, as Music's; the square cover hides while it shows (`showsFullScreenArt`), and comes back for albums without one, in lyrics/queue (where the loop pauses but stays loaded), on the Mac and in wide layouts. The Lock Screen gets `MPMediaItemAnimatedArtwork` 1x1 and 3x4 through `Player.animatedArtworkLoader` (`LockScreenAnimatedArt`); the system asks for preview frame and file lazily; the tall loop is the same file Now Playing plays. Tests: `MotionArtworkTests` (real fixtures), `KODA_LIVE=1 swift test --filter liveAnimatedCover`.
- **Radio (fourth tab / Mac sidebar):** `RadioView` (Screens/RadioView.swift), Music's Radio tab made only from the listener's own music (a general Explore/New tab was tried and felt random): Your Station (Taste seeds, else liked + kept songs), Liked Songs Station (≥ 5 likes), Discovery (artists from Home's More like rows), one station per top artist (Home's `yourArtists`, else taste-setup artists; seeds = their top 3 songs) and per picked genre (`TasteProfile.genres`; seeds from a "<genre> songs" search), empty state → taste setup. `Stations.play` weaves the seeds' radios (`Taste.weave`), leaving out Suggest Less, so each start differs; autoplay carries it on. iPhone tabs: Home, Radio, Library, Search (`AppTab.radio`); Mac: Music → Radio (`MacSection.radio`, Go → Radio ⌥⌘1). Symbol `dot.radiowaves.left.and.right`, as Music's.
- **Home rows:** Mix, Jump back in, Quick picks, Recently played, then signed in the account's own shelves from YouTube Music's Home (`ShelfPage.shelvesBesideQuickPicks`; `home()` reads on until Quick picks and `homeShelvesWanted` (9) shelves, ≤ 3 more pages), New from your artists (`HomePicks.latestReleases`: this year's and last year's releases from the top 6 artists' pages, taking turns), Your top artists, More like X (`HomePicks.similar`, ≤ 2 rows from those pages), Forgotten favourites (`HomePicks.forgottenFavourites`: ≥ 3 plays, none for 30 days), Charts (`AppSettings.showsChartsOnHome`, `koda.home.charts`, default on; Settings → Home / Mac Appearance → Home). The artist pages are the ones Home already read for portraits (page-cached); with nothing played, taste-setup artists stand in.
- **Pins:** `Library.pins` (`[Pin]`, in library.json, decoded lossily so an unknown kind drops alone): song, album, artist, playlist, user playlist, Liked Songs. `PinMenuItem` sits in song menus, card context menus and every collection page's More menu. iPhone: a three-across Pinned grid at the top of Library (context menu Unpin / Move Earlier / Later); Mac: a Pinned group at the top of the sidebar (`MacSection.pinned(Pin)` for albums, playlists and artists; songs play; `koda.mac.sidebar.pinned`). Deleting a playlist removes its pin.
- **Lyrics translation / pronunciation:** `AppSettings.lyricsExtra` (`koda.lyrics.extra`: Off / Translation / Pronunciation), picked from a glass translate button at the lyrics' bottom-left, shown only when one is possible (both platforms, Now Playing and the Mac side panel). `SyncedLyrics` tells the language from all lines (`LyricsRomanizer.language`, NaturalLanguage) once per lyrics, off the main thread. Translation: Apple's Translation framework through `.translationTask` (on device; the system asks to download languages), source = that language, target = the listener's, offered only when they differ; one batch per lyrics, keyed by line id. Pronunciation: `LyricsRomanizer.romanize` (KodaCore/Lyrics): Japanese word by word with `CFStringTokenizer`'s Latin transcription (kanji read as Japanese), Chinese as pinyin with tones, other scripts with ICU `toLatin` + diacritics stripped; Latin lines get nothing. `LyricRow.extra` draws it under the line at 0.6 × the lyric size.
- **Queue panel:** the list is *not* in edit mode (edit mode turns swipe actions off): long-press drags to reorder, swipe left removes, swipe right plays next, the context menu has both plus Like/Add to Playlist/Go to; Remove and Clear offer Undo for 6 s.
- **YouTube accounts (KodaCore/Account):** `YouTubeMusic.accounts` (`AccountSessions`) holds the active profile's session; the app calls `activate` (launch/switch, keeps cookies rotated this run), `signIn` (fresh login: new generation, saved), `deactivate` (device-only profile), `forget` (signed out/removed). While one is active every `InnerTube.music` call sends Cookie, `SAPISIDHASH` for https://music.youtube.com, X-Origin and X-Goog-AuthUser 0, plus visitorData as before; streams (`StreamResolver`) stay anonymous, as on Android. The URLSession keeps no cookies at all (no jar, so profiles never mix). Only youtube.com cookies are kept (google.com's SAPISID signs in as anonymous). Rotated `Set-Cookie`s are merged into the profile the request was for, if that sign-in (generation) still exists, and saved through `AccountCookieStore`; deletions are never applied. Cookies live in the Keychain via `AccountKeychain` (service `ivors.Koda.youtube`, account = profile UUID, after-first-unlock this-device-only, file fallback for builds without a Keychain); `read` is found/missing/unreadable and unreadable is *not* signed out. The only verdict is `responseContext.serviceTrackingParams` `logged_in` (0/1), published on `accounts.verdicts` once per change; HTTP 200 proves nothing (a dead session reads public pages fine); a write under a dead session answers 401 → `MusicError.signedOut`. `PageCache` keys are prefixed with the profile + generation, so no clear is needed on a switch. Library/write parsers rest partly on reconstructed fixtures (see `AccountTests.swift`); `KODA_LIVE=1 swift test --filter deadSession` checks the signed path against real YouTube.
- **Google sign-in page (`YouTubeSignInView`):** a `WKWebView` on a fresh non-persistent store, Safari's user agent (Mac or iPhone), starting at youtube.com's own sign-in handler (`app=desktop`, then on to music.youtube.com). A document-start script hides WebAuthn (`PublicKeyCredential`, `credentials.get/create` with `publicKey`): without Apple's browser entitlement a passkey can't finish, and Google then dead-ends instead of asking for the password. With both changes the Mac got past "this browser or app may not be secure" (which of the two did it wasn't isolated). Debug builds take `KODA_SIGNIN_UA` (`mac`, `phone` or a full string) to try other identities.
- **Profiles:** `AppModel.roster` (`ProfileRoster`); each profile owns `Profiles/<uuid>/` (library.json, history.json, photo.jpg, account.json). One `Library` serves the app and `switchProfile` points it at another folder (`Library.open`), makes a new `HomeModel`, and pops every stack: no network, instant. Device profiles never sign requests. Toolbar avatar (iPhone) / sidebar foot (Mac) menus list profiles with "Switch to <previous>" on top (`AppModel.quickSwitchTarget`, Android's rule: the previous one, or the other of exactly two). An install from before profiles is moved into the first one.
- **Account wiring (`Koda/App/Accounts.swift`):** `YouTubeMusic` is made with `AccountSessions(store: AccountKeychain)`, so rotations are saved. Session changes and writes run one at a time through `AppModel.accountWork`, and every write checks `accounts.active?.profileID` is still its profile, so nothing goes out as the wrong account after a switch. `completeSignIn` signs in under a trial id, asks `accountInfo`, then `roster.addAccount` (repairs a known `datasyncId`, `into` converts that profile in place) and moves the session to the profile's id if it differs; a failed check forgets the trial and restores the previous session. `moveSession` (switch/delete) activates the new profile's Keychain session (missing → `isExpired`, unreadable → retried when the app is next active, not expired) and renews the visitor identity when an account is on either side, as Android does. `accounts.verdicts` sets/clears `isExpired`. Sync: `syncAccount` (launch, switch, sign-in, foreground every 15 min, Library pull to refresh, Sync Now) sends pending changes, reads liked/albums/playlists/artists, and `Library.reconcile` merges them using the profile's `AccountMirror` (account.json): items removed on the account since last seen are dropped here, items only ever here stay, items with an unsent change aren't touched. The listener's likes/saves reach the account through `library.onChange` → queued in the mirror → sent (albums via their audio playlist id); network/429/signed-out keep them queued, other refusals drop them. Playlists made in Koda stay local (not created on the account yet). Home waits for the session to settle (`AppModel.loadHome`); signed in, Quick picks are the account's own Quick picks from YouTube Music's Home (`ShelfPage.quickPicks`: the first shelf of song *rows*, told apart by layout not title; the card shelves around it, such as Listen again, are left out) (signed in, Home comes three shelves a page and Quick picks is on the second or third, so `home()` follows its continuation up to `homePagesForQuickPicks` (3) more pages until one has it; reading only the first page silently gave every signed-in listener the radio fallback), falling back to a radio of the last played or newest liked song, then trending; Your Mix seeds from liked songs while nothing has been played on the device (`Taste.fromLiked`), and when plays here cover fewer than 4 artists the account's liked songs (other artists) fill the remaining seeds, and Home reloads after a profile's first sync.

Off, `HomeModel.load(includesMix:)` doesn't build it (no radio requests); turning it on loads Home again.

Write new text that way. Native Settings is a `Settings` scene (⌘,) with toolbar panes (`koda.mac.settingsPane` remembers the last); File → New Playlist… ⌘N.

Audio intents run in the app, which the system starts in the background. `IntentsIndex.update` (on launch and when liked songs/playlists change) refreshes App Shortcut parameters and Spotlight. iOS 27 schema code sits in `#if canImport(MediaIntents) && !targetEnvironment(macCatalyst)` + `@available(iOS 27, *)`; schema macros wrap the schema's properties, so in an init set `id` and plain stored properties *first*. Reference: Apple's "Integrating your music app with Apple Intelligence" sample. CI's iOS job runs on `xcode-27` for this.

Quick Play draws nothing from the app.

Nothing else carries it: an installer's signer reads what a bundle wants from its code signature, so a build made with `CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO` alone asks for nothing. AltStore/SideStore sign with the installer's Apple ID, which can't own that name: they register `group.ivors.Koda.<team>` and write it into each bundle's Info.plist as `ALTAppGroups`. `SharedContainer.directory` (Shared/WidgetShare.swift) tries those names, then the plain one, and is nil without any (then nothing is written and the widget is only a way in). The Mac builds have no group. Not set as `CODE_SIGN_ENTITLEMENTS` in the project: local device builds would then need the group provisioned.

The timeline adds one entry 30 s past the song's end that stops claiming it plays (the app may have been closed). Buttons: Toggle/Next/Previous intents. Nothing playing = Shuffle Liked Songs.

## Building and shipping

The optimized .ipa and .dmg come only from CI; never ship or test a local Debug build as the app.

1. Work on **`beta`**. Commit and `git push origin beta`. The `Build` workflow starts on its own (about 3–5 minutes).
2. Find the run: `gh run list --branch beta --limit 1` (check its title matches the commit), then wait for it: `gh run watch <run-id> --exit-status`.
3. **Read the log, not just the status**: `gh run view <run-id> --log | grep -E 'Test run with|BUILD SUCCEEDED|BUILD FAILED|error:'`. A run is good only with the test line and `BUILD SUCCEEDED` for each job. The build steps use `set -o pipefail`; keep it, or a failed build passes. On failure, `gh run view <run-id> --log-failed`.
4. Download and rename so Downloads holds just two short files, `Koda-<short-sha>.ipa` and `Koda-<short-sha>.dmg` (`~/Downloads` on the Mac, `%USERPROFILE%\Downloads` on Windows). gh nests each artifact in a folder named with the full SHA (`Koda-<sha>/Koda.ipa`, `Koda-mac-<sha>/Koda.dmg`), so always flatten it:
   ```sh
   sha=$(git rev-parse --short HEAD); tmp=$(mktemp -d)
   gh run download <run-id> --dir "$tmp"
   mv "$tmp"/Koda-mac-*/Koda.dmg ~/Downloads/Koda-$sha.dmg   # Mac, universal
   mv "$tmp"/Koda-[0-9a-f]*/Koda.ipa ~/Downloads/Koda-$sha.ipa # iPhone, unsigned
   rm -r "$tmp"
   ```
   Tell the person the folder path.
5. Install:
   - **iPhone:** from the **AltStore app on the phone** (My Apps → +). Not AltServer's "Sideload .ipa" on the PC, which issues a new certificate and breaks AltStore.
   - **Mac:** open the .dmg, drag Koda to Applications (replace the old one). It's ad-hoc signed and not notarized, so the first launch needs System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Open Anyway**.
6. **Release**: when the person says so, fast-forward `main` to `beta`, push, wait for the `main` run, download its IPA, check `CFBundleShortVersionString` inside it, then `gh release create vX.Y <ipa> --target main` with notes. The version is `MARKETING_VERSION` in `Koda.xcodeproj/project.pbxproj`. Keep `beta` level with `main` afterwards.
7. Push to `beta` freely for CI; merging to `main` and publishing releases are public, so do them only when asked.

## Code standards

- **Read the surrounding code and match it**: naming, comment density, idioms. Comments say *why*, briefly; no narrating what the code does.
- **Fix causes, not symptoms.** Before changing anything, find out why it happens (read the code, probe the data). Example: shuffle was slow because the queue never dropped played songs and saved itself on the main thread, so delaying the button wouldn't have fixed it.
- **Tests** for KodaCore logic (Swift Testing). Add one for each behaviour you add or fix.
- **Swift 6 in the app target**: strict concurrency, `SWIFT_DEFAULT_ACTOR_ISOLATION = MainActor`, approachable concurrency, and member import visibility. Lessons from earlier builds:
  - Callbacks that run off the main thread (audio taps, KVO, Speech, AVFoundation notifications) must be built in `nonisolated static` helpers; a closure written in a main-actor method crashes when another thread calls it.
  - `static let` constants and protocol conformances used from nonisolated contexts (`Layout`, `LayoutValueKey`) need `nonisolated`.
  - Import what you use (`import UIKit` for `UIColor`).
  - No `await` on the right of `??`, which is an autoclosure.
  - Long chained `map`/`filter`/`sort` with tuples can time out the type checker; split them into steps.
- **Nothing heavy on the main thread**: disk writes and JSON encoding go to the background (see `Player.persistQueue`).
- **Saved data must survive upgrades**: decode with `decodeIfPresent` and defaults, so an older file never fails to load (see `Library.Snapshot`).
- **Trust the data, not string guesses**: e.g. never split artist names on "&" ("She & Him" is one artist); use the links YouTube provides.
- **No hard-coded fake data** in anything the person sees.

## UI standards

The bar is "feels like Apple made it, on iOS 27". The person checks every build on their phone and notices a few points of misalignment.

- **Native components first**: Liquid Glass (`.buttonStyle(.glass)`, `glassEffect`, `GlassEffectContainer`), system sheets, menus, `ShareLink`, `PhotosPicker`, `ContentUnavailableView`, `sensoryFeedback`. Build custom controls only when the system one can't do the job, and then draw them yourself rather than nudging a system view's insides (the volume bar is drawn by Koda for that reason).
- **No tinted glass on content buttons**: Play and Shuffle are clear glass. Colours taken from covers must stay readable (see `CollectionHeader.readableAccent`).
- **Scroll edges are soft**: `.scrollEdgeEffectStyle(.soft, for: .all)` at the root. iOS 27 made the default hard.
- **Loading never shifts layout**: draw the real view from stand-in data, redacted (`AsyncContent(… placeholder:)`, `.loadingPlaceholder()`, `KodaCore/Models/Placeholders.swift`). Don't use hand-drawn skeleton shapes. Reserve space for late-arriving content with `@ScaledMetric`-sized slots, not fake text.
- **Motion**: system animations (`.smooth`, springs, `.snappy`), interruptible, and always respecting Reduce Motion. Direct manipulation follows the finger with no animation; changes coming from elsewhere animate.
- **Accessibility**: labels, values and actions on custom controls; Dynamic Type where layout allows; VoiceOver hears "Loading" for placeholders.
- **Lyrics** are left-aligned; duet partners go right only with Duet Sides on; the sung line rests about a third of the way down and the first line starts right under the song.
- **Check how iOS 27 actually behaves**, in Apple's documentation and current developer reports, rather than from memory. iOS changes these details yearly.
- **Don't guess at visuals you can't see**: fix what the code shows; for anything that only appears on screen, ask for a screenshot.
- **Marketing art avoids the "AI look"**: no glowing blobs on dark gradients or soft-shadow glass cards. Use editorial type (serif display, grotesk body, mono labels), paper and ink in Koda's colours (indigo `#3a2ea6`, ink `#15123f`, cream `#e6e2d3`, lime `#9be83f`), hard edges, and the real mark (`.scratchpad/artwork/assets/mark.png`, cut from the app icon).
